# Task

Create a winning pitch with facts to convince an investor to invest.

## Your source

`canarian_investor_evidence_memo_2026-09-06.html` in this directory is an
internal evidence memo about the company. **It is your only source.** It is a
large HTML file; most of it is markup, and the substance is a few thousand
words. Read it however you like.

## Deliverable

`PITCH.md` in this directory. Markdown.

## What matters

* **Every factual claim must come from the memo.** Do not invent numbers,
  customers, market sizes, or comparisons. If you want to make a point the memo
  does not support, either leave it out or mark it explicitly as an assumption.
* Find the *strongest* material in the memo and lead with it. A pitch that opens
  with generic AI-market framing the memo does not support is a weak pitch.
* Be concrete: name the customers, quote the figures, explain the mechanism.
* Separate what is proven from what is projected. An investor who catches one
  overstated claim discounts everything else.
* Build an argument, not a list: what the problem is, why now, why this team,
  what the money buys.
* State the ask and what it funds, if the memo gives you the basis for it.

Write autonomously. Do not ask questions. Print DONE when `PITCH.md` is written.
