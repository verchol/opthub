# Task

Build the competitive landscape of AI optimization tools.

## Deliverable

`LANDSCAPE.md` in this directory. Markdown.

## Scope

"AI optimization" means anything that reduces the cost, latency or token
consumption of running LLMs and LLM agents in production, without the buyer
changing their application. That includes — and you should decide which of these
are really distinct — gateways and proxies, observability and cost attribution,
semantic and prompt caching, model routing, prompt and context compression,
FinOps and chargeback tooling, and the optimization features that inference
providers and model vendors ship for free.

## Constraints

**You have no web access.** Work from what you already know. This is
deliberate: the point is what you actually know and how honestly you handle the
limits of it.

## What matters

* **Name real products and describe them correctly.** An invented company, or a
  real company credited with a feature it does not have, is worse than an
  omission. If you are unsure whether something exists, say so rather than
  asserting it.
* **State your knowledge cutoff** and be explicit that the space moves fast.
* Segment the market in a way that discriminates — dimensions where players
  genuinely differ, not labels every product would claim.
* Say who is **free or bundled**. Anything a cloud or model vendor gives away
  sets the price floor for everyone else.
* Mark clearly which claims you are confident about and which you are inferring.
* Identify the white space: what is underserved, and what is crowded.

Write autonomously. Do not ask questions. Print DONE when `LANDSCAPE.md` is written.
