# Task

Create an AI optimization strategy document that provides all potential
optimization aspects and chance to see wins without compromise on accuracy.

## Deliverable

A single markdown file, `STRATEGY.md`, in this directory.

## What the document is for

A team is running coding agents in production and their spend is growing faster
than their headcount. They need to know **every lever available to them**, what
each one is likely to be worth, and — critically — which ones risk degrading
output quality and which do not.

The document is read by an engineering lead who has to choose what to do first
and defend that choice.

## Requirements

* Cover the full space of optimization levers, not a favourite few.
* For each lever: what it is, the mechanism by which it saves, the rough
  magnitude of the win, and its **accuracy risk** stated honestly.
* Separate levers that are effectively free of accuracy risk from those that
  trade quality for cost. A reader must be able to tell them apart at a glance.
* Be concrete. Name techniques, settings, and numbers rather than gesturing at
  categories.
* Say how each lever would be measured, so a claim can be checked.
* Give a prioritised sequence: what to do first, and why.
* Where a lever's value depends on the workload, say what it depends on.

Write autonomously. Do not ask questions. Print DONE when the file is written.
